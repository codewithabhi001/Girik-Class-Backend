GR Class Certificate Developer Tags Reference
=============================================

Below is the list of all dynamic placeholder tags (developer tags) currently supported when generating certificates in the GR Class backend system.

1. STANDARD VESSEL & CERTIFICATE PARTICULARS:
* certificate_number: Unique identifier for the certificate
  Matches/Synonyms: "certificate number", "no de certificado", "certificate no", "certificado no.", "certificatenumber", "certnumber", "certno"
  
* vessel_name: Name of the vessel / ship
  Matches/Synonyms: "name of ship", "name of vessel", "vesselname", "nameofship", "shipname", "nombredelanave", "vessel"
  
* imo_number: IMO Ship Identification Number
  Matches/Synonyms: "imo number", "imo number1", "imo no.", "imo no", "imonumber", "imo", "imono"
  
* call_sign: Distinctive call letters or signal
  Matches/Synonyms: "distinctive number or letters", "call sign", "señal distintiva", "callsign", "distinctivenumber", "sign"
  
* port_of_registry: Home port of registry
  Matches/Synonyms: "port of registry", "puerto de registro", "portofregistry", "registryport", "puertoderegistro"
  
* gross_tonnage: Gross Tonnage (GT)
  Matches/Synonyms: "gross tonnage", "arqueo bruto", "grosstonnage", "gt"
  
* net_tonnage: Net Tonnage (NT)
  Matches/Synonyms: "net tonnage", "arqueo neto", "nettonnage", "nt"
  
* deadweight: Deadweight Tonnage (DWT)
  Matches/Synonyms: "deadweight", "peso muerto", "dwt"
  
* year_built: Date keel was laid / year built
  Matches/Synonyms: "date of construction", "date of built", "date of build", "keel laid", "año de construcción", "fechadeconstruccion", "yearbuilt", "built"
  
* ship_type: Type of vessel classification
  Matches/Synonyms: "ship type", "tipo de buque", "type of ship", "shiptype", "vesseltype"


2. DATES & LOCATIONS:
* issue_date: Date the certificate is officially issued
  Matches/Synonyms: "date of issue", "fecha de emision", "fecha de emisión", "issuedate"
  
* expiry_date: Date of certificate expiration
  Matches/Synonyms: "valid until", "valido hasta", "expirydate", "validity", "validuntil"
  
* survey_completion_date: Date the survey was completed
  Matches/Synonyms: "completion date of the survey", "fecha de terminación", "surveycompletiondate", "surveydate"
  
* place_of_survey: Place/Port where certificate is issued / survey completed
  Matches/Synonyms: "issued at", "expedido en", "lugar de emision", "port", "place", "location", "placeofsurvey"
  
* surveyor_name: Full name of the inspector / auditor
  Matches/Synonyms: "surveyor name", "nombre del inspector", "auditor name", "surveyorname", "surveyor"
  
* flag_state: Flag administration governing the ship
  Matches/Synonyms: "flag state", "flag", "flagstate"


3. ADDITIONAL FIELDS:
* owner_operators: Client / Owner / Operator company name
  Matches/Synonyms: "owner", "operator", "company", "client", "owneroperator"
  
* mmsi_number: Maritime Mobile Service Identity number
  Matches/Synonyms: "mmsi_number"
  
* ballast_water_capacity: Ballast Water Capacity in cubic meters
  Matches/Synonyms: "ballast water capacity", "capacidad de agua de lastre"
  
* certificate_type: Certificate Type Name
  Matches/Synonyms: "certificate_type", "certtype", "certname"
  
* certificate_term: Term of certificate (FULL_TERM / SHORT_TERM)
  Matches/Synonyms: "certificate_term", "term"
  
* gr_class_logo: URL/Base64 of GR Class Logo
  Matches/Synonyms: "gr_class_logo"
  
* flag_logo: URL/Base64 of Flag State logo
  Matches/Synonyms: "flag_logo"

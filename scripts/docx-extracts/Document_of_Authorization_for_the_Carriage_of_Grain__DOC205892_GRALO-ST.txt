CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL







The Master shall be guided by the instructions and arrangements set forth in “GRAIN LOADING MANUAL”.

The above manual contains data for full holds FULLY TRIMMED only and this approval applies only in respect of loadings with full holds so trimmed.

The Master shall satisfy himself by calculations prior to going to sea and by using the data contained in the above Manual that the vessel at any stage in the proposed voyage complies with the requirements of Chapter VI of the International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea 1974.

This Certificate and the GRAIN LOADING MANUAL are to be kept on board the vessel and, if so required, shall be presented for the inspection by the appropriate Authority at ports of loading together with any calculations required by Item 3 above.

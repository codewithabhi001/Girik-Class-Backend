ADVISE TO OWNERS

According to GR CLASS Rules, and Flag Administration provisions and requirements:

  1. The class will be AUTOMATICALLY suspended:
    • In case of Damage of the ship.
    • In case of every transgressing the specified service conditions without GR CLASS agreement.
    • If surveys towards Class Renewal Survey have not been completed.
    • If the annual/intermediate survey is not completed within 3 months after due date.
  2. The Class will be subject of suspension:
    • If items covered by continuous system are not surveyed within due date.
    • If conditions and recommendations are not dealt with within the due date.
    • If voyage repairs are performed without prior GR CLASS Agreement.
    • In case of Owners financial overdue.
